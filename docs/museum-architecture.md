# Museum Architecture

Status: **concept approved; low-poly blockout awaiting user review**.

## Concept

The initial direction draws from contemporary West African and Sahelian spatial principles without
copying culture-specific ornament: protective earthen massing, deep shade, courtyard-like rhythm,
framed openings, and controlled daylight. The experience is a sequence of changing scale rather
than decoration.

One Three.js world unit represents one metre. Visitors enter from the south and travel north:

| Space              | Width | Depth | Height |
| ------------------ | ----: | ----: | -----: |
| Entrance lobby     |  18 m |  16 m |    9 m |
| Connecting passage |   6 m |  13 m |    6 m |
| Circular forum     |  44 m |  44 m |   15 m |

The entrance opening is proposed at 5 m wide by 7 m high. The camera begins at `(0, 1.7, 25)` and
faces the lobby. A narrow passage creates compression before the main hall opens into a circular
forum. Its outer perimeter is reserved for future exhibits; no stands, plaques, or artifacts are
included. Four concentric floor levels descend 1.6 m toward a seven-metre-radius central floor.

## Lighting concept

The lobby receives broad light through the entrance and a linear roof slot. The passage is lower
and quieter. Following the second user reference, the hall is now a brick-toned rotunda organized
around a central skylight. Sixteen columns support a ring balcony; spaced upper screen segments
produce a perforated enclosure and shadow rhythm. Brick, dark steel, textured concrete, and glass
remain material candidates rather than final assets.

The concept was approved on 2026-11-06. The current implementation deliberately uses primitive box
geometry and neutral materials to test proportions. Entrance massing, room volumes, openings,
forum tiers, balcony, screen density, skylight, and roof geometry remain revisable until the complete
blockout is approved.
